1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the MoldTrend Research project and this website. MoldTrend Research is an internal, read-only research tool that studies design-element and product-form trends for DIY silicone and resin molds. It is not a public service: there are no user accounts, no sign-ups and no transactions.

2. This website

  • This site is a static purpose statement. It sets no cookies, runs no JavaScript, uses no third-party resources and has no analytics or tracking.
  • We do not ask for, collect or store personal information through this site. There is no contact form, no newsletter and no download.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request data (such as an IP address in server logs) under its own privacy statement. We do not receive or use that data.
  • The only way to contact us from this site is by sending an email to the address below.

3. What the research tool processes

  • Public, non-personal information only: publicly available market-interest signals (for example keyword search interest) and public listing metadata obtained through official platform APIs.
  • No member personal data: we do not collect, store, share or process personal information about platform members, buyers or sellers. We do not access member-only, private or login-gated areas, and we do not collect member IDs, passwords or contact details.
  • No credentials from third parties: the tool uses only the operator's own API credentials, which are kept locally by the operator and are never published, shared or embedded in this site.
  • No personal data is sold or shared: we do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise disclose data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. What is stored, and for how long

  • Source content obtained from a platform API is retained only for the minimum period that the platform's terms require, and is then deleted or stripped of content.
  • Derived statistics that we compute ourselves (aggregated counts and our own category labels) may be kept longer, because they contain no platform content and no personal data.
  • Nothing on this website contains collected data of any kind.

5. Your choices and rights

Because this site collects no personal data and the tool processes no member personal data, there is no personal profile to access, correct or delete. If you believe that any information relating to you has been processed in error, please contact us and we will investigate and, where appropriate, delete it.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the project changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly. Continued use of this site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

7. Contact
